Highlights
Among the reported diseases, Number
of SARI Cases Reported is the leading
cause of morbidity in Week 51 with 447
reported cases.
Overall reporting rate of Week 51 is
95.5% with 128 out of 134 sentinel sites
reporting to EWARS.
This bulletin includes data since January 2023 till date, including comparison of the same period last two
year. In May 2019, 36 new sentinel sites (including public hospitals, private hospitals, and medical colleges)
were added and was gradually oriented and started reporting. Recently, in November 2024, 17 reporting sites
selected from all the provinces of Nepal were upgraded to sentinel sites, thus the number of sentinel sites
have increased. The current number of sentinel sites is 134.

Summary Observation (including probable and suspect cases)
Most of the AGE cases were reported from PARSA (20 cases), KATHMANDU (18 cases),
KAVREPALANCHOK (17 cases), KAILALI (13 cases) and LALITPUR (13 cases).
Most of the SARI cases were reported from MORANG (40 cases), KAILALI (34 cases), KATHMANDU
(34 cases), RUPANDEHI (28 cases) and CHITAWAN (16 cases).
Cholera case(s) were reported from SIRAHA (1 case).
Malaria case(s) were reported from GORKHA (1 case).
Most of the Dengue cases were reported from KATHMANDU (8 cases), LAMJUNG (7 cases),
NAWALPARASI WEST (3 cases), JHAPA (3 cases) and DOLAKHA (2 cases).
